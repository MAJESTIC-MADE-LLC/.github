## Turnstamp approved enhancements — November 4, 2026

Turnstamp remains **concept-only at position 8, after Daymerit**. The approved
[enhancement specification](https://github.com/MAJESTIC-MADE-LLC/Turnstamp/blob/main/docs/ENHANCEMENTS.md)
requires all six capabilities for its first iOS/Android release: reusable property/visit scope,
paired before/after capture with an alignment overlay, scope exceptions, branded selective PDFs,
complaint/re-clean history and offline capture through report generation. Include residential,
deep-clean, move-out and turnover templates. Preserve original photographs and finalized report
versions separately from annotations, revisions and re-cleans; private notes/imported images are
excluded from reports by default and location collection starts off.

The [acceptance checklist](https://github.com/MAJESTIC-MADE-LLC/Turnstamp/blob/main/docs/ACCEPTANCE.md)
requires five target cleaners using six rooms, 12 photo pairs and two exceptions: at least four
must approve a generated report within two minutes after documentation, without assistance or
accidental disclosure. This target is unvalidated and does not establish a “fastest” claim.
Qualify photo-heavy encrypted recovery, bounded offline entitlement and physical/store behavior.
Keep **$9.99/month or $79.99/year** with the approved 14-day local trial from first meaningful use,
without payment details or automatic billing. All six workflows, branding and essential layouts
are included without per-property or per-report charges.

Later [optional own-device hosting](https://github.com/MAJESTIC-MADE-LLC/Turnstamp/blob/main/docs/USER_OWNED_HOSTING.md)
follows shared reference-client and Pantry Clock pilot qualification. Select property/job
collections for the cleaner's own approved devices through a Mac/Windows host over LAN/private VPN;
keep sync consent separate from PDF selections. The [latest shared hosting contracts](USER_OWNED_HOSTING.md)
govern implementation: the storage daemon remains ciphertext-only, while a separately enrolled
coordinator is an explicitly trusted plaintext endpoint for selected collections. This expansion
adds no first-release prerequisite or new pricing. Helpers, client accounts/portals, hosted client
report links, scheduling, invoicing and dispatch remain deferred. No app or host implementation,
release qualification or deployment is established by recording these requirements.

## How to use the queue

- First pass the [shared foundation adoption and qualification gate](STACK_STANDARD.md). Then finish
  and record the current product's applicable [release gates](RELEASE_DEFINITION.md)
  before moving to the next app. Record any owner/device/service blocker; do not silently skip or
  reorder the queue. Support and documentation work may unblock the current app.
- After the common foundation qualifies across the template and all seven existing apps, each app
  can receive its own product release approval. The foundation gate is not a requirement for all
  seven products to finish every domain/legal/accessibility/store-submission check. Publication and
  paid-service changes remain separate owner-controlled actions.
- All seven apps implement the shared local foundation and pass local tests/both native builds.
  Daymerit is account-free/local; Namefold's native wallet is encrypted and its separate static PWA
  stores browser drafts/backups unencrypted. Hosted source/reports remain archived/historical.
  Physical recovery/privacy, native-store acceptance and immutable-package provenance remain pending.
- Consumer records stay on-device by default. Exports are deliberate user actions. Optional
  user-owned hosting is [approved future design](USER_OWNED_HOSTING.md), not an implemented feature
  or new launch gate. Its reference/Pantry Clock pilot sequence orders future hosting engineering,
  not public releases. Scoped sync enrollment will authorize recurring transfers explicitly.
  Company-hosted record storage is not equivalent to customer self-hosting.
- The November 4 [subscription policy and prices](PRICING.md) supersede permanent-core sales.
  Most current adapters still need migration; Skepbook's monthly/yearly source is present. Configure and
  qualify monthly/annual products, renewal/expiration/restore and bounded offline access separately.
  Preserve existing records, export, encrypted recovery and authentic legacy ownership.
- Quiet Arcade is a Hub concept without a repo. Its slot does not authorize repository creation.
  Lockbox's autonomous time-release/dead-man delivery is deferred; the queued scope is a document
  vault and explicit encrypted handoff.
