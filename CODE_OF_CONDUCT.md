# Community Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Focus criticism on the work, welcome questions, and
protect other people's privacy. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate
disruption, impersonation, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

These standards apply in Majestic Made repositories and in public spaces where someone is acting on
behalf of the organization.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `support@majesticmade.dev` with `[CONDUCT]` at the start of the
subject. Include links or concise evidence, but do not forward unrelated private data. Reports will
be reviewed as privately as reasonably possible. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and
may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants based on the severity and pattern of
the conduct.
